FAQs

Who can become a partner?

We welcome businesses in Canada and the United States that want to support bison conservation.

How do we join?

Contact us with your business name and the tier you’re interested in. We’ll follow up with participation details.

Who chooses which projects receive support?

The Alliance selects projects in consultation with the people leading the conservation work. Partner tiers determine recognition and benefits.

Can partners use the Alliance seal?

Tier-specific seals are included in the proposed benefits. Contact the Alliance for approved artwork and usage guidelines.
